EES Work bundled font notices
===============================

The EES theme uses byte-for-byte copies of the font files already included
in the pinned official Open WebUI wheel. Font binaries and their embedded
names, copyright notices, and license metadata are not modified.

Source wheel: open_webui-0.11.3-py3-none-any.whl
Source wheel SHA256:
8436f9bb29c5accbdfd90d78470fcc917c882bd53f72ed88fed91b1ee97fa547

"EES Inter" and "EES Noto Sans KR" are CSS font-family aliases used to scope
the theme's font-face declarations. They do not rename the font binaries.
